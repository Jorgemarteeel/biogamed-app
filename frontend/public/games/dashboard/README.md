# Ilustraciones minimalistas del panel

Imágenes originales generadas el 25 de noviembre de 2026 con la herramienta integrada image_gen y seleccionadas tras la petición de simplificar el estilo. Se reemplazó la primera propuesta 3D por formas planas, menos elementos y una paleta azul, naranja y verde azulado común.

- `pasapalabra-minimal.png`: rosco ilustrativo de letras.
- `trivial-minimal.png`: recorrido en espiral, dos fichas y un dado.

Son imágenes de presentación, no la representación exacta del rosco operativo ni de las 140 casillas del tablero. La pista, las descripciones y las acciones son HTML accesible. Next Image adapta su tamaño de entrega. No dependen de archivos privados ni contienen material docente original.

## Prompts finales

### Pasapalabra

Create an original minimalist flat editorial illustration for BioGaMed's Pasapalabra game card. Wide landscape 3:2 image. Extremely simple clean 2D vector-style shapes rendered as a raster image, on a uniform very pale blue background #EFF5FB. Centered circular ring of small navy blue #172554 discs with a single clear uppercase white letter on each disc, forming a recognizable word quiz wheel. Most discs navy, exactly two teal #0D9488 and one orange #EA580C accents. Generous blank center and generous outer whitespace. Use only the letter ring: no clock, no cards, no question mark, no other objects, no decorative particles. Thin clean forms, no 3D, no gradients, no textures, no bevels, no lighting, no drop shadows. Crisp restrained university educational app visual, not a toy. No headings, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Subject occupies 65 percent of frame height and is fully within frame. The final image must be legible when shown at 350px wide.

### Trivial

Create an original minimalist flat editorial illustration for BioGaMed's Trivial board game card, matching a simple navy alphabet quiz wheel illustration. Wide landscape 3:2 image on a uniform very pale blue background #EFF5FB. Strictly 2D overhead view, simple geometric shapes. A small clean winding route of rounded square cells curling toward the center, mostly pale blue and navy #172554 with a few teal #0D9488 and orange #EA580C cells. Two tiny simple pawn silhouettes, one navy one orange, on two separate cells. A single small white rounded square die with three navy pips to the lower right of the route. Optional tiny orange triangular finish flag in the center, no pole embellishments. Lots of whitespace; route and objects occupy 65 percent of frame height. No board frame, no cards, no text, no numbers, no logos, no decorative background shapes, no watermark. No 3D, no isometric perspective, no gradients, no texture, no bevels, no shadows. Clean understated vector-style raster illustration, mature academic app.
